The cost is in the unstructured work — the email triage, the document review, the back-and-forth that happens before a deal, claim, or case gets resolved. Here's where we've seen agents pay back in each industry. And five horizontals that cut across all of them.

V-01

Legal

Practices spend 40%+ of associate time on unstructured input. The agent surface here is wide.

  • Contract reviewRedline against playbook, flag deviations, draft markup with citations to clause.
  • Matter intakeNew-matter forms drafted from inbound email + conflicts check; confirmation back to client in minutes.
  • Doc summarizationDiscovery batches, depositions, exhibits — summarized with page-level citations for verification.
  • Billing narrativesDraft narrative descriptions from time entries; reviewed by the attorney before invoice.
V-02

Accounting
& Finance

Deterministic where possible, AI where the input is messy. Always with the audit trail intact.

  • Invoice processingPO matching, GL coding, exception flagging — deposited into your accounting system, reviewable.
  • Reconciliation prepFirst-pass reconciliation surfaces only the discrepancies for human review. No more line-by-line.
  • Client commsDrafted responses to common client questions, grounded in their actual file.
  • Tax prep triageInbound document classification, missing-item chasing, organizer pre-fill.
V-03

Healthcare

Privacy and compliance shape the architecture. Always HITL on clinical output. Nothing leaves the perimeter unreviewed.

  • Patient intakeForms transcribed, history summarized, charts pre-populated for the provider.
  • Referral processingInbound referral docs parsed, scheduled, and acknowledged automatically.
  • Clinical notesNote drafting from dictation or visit transcript. Provider edits and signs.
  • SchedulingInbox-driven scheduling and reschedule handling. Reduces no-shows materially.
V-04

Insurance

Triage, extraction, and decisioning support — never decisioning. The carrier still owns the call.

  • Claims triageSeverity, coverage, fraud signals — initial assessment to route to the right adjuster.
  • Policy Q&AInsureds and brokers get accurate, citation-backed answers from the actual policy doc.
  • UnderwritingData extraction from inbound submissions; structured input ready for the underwriter.
  • RenewalsRenewal package preparation, exception flagging, broker comms.
V-05

Logistics
& Trucking

High volume, narrow language, well-defined inputs. The category that pencils out fastest.

  • Load tendersInbound tender emails parsed, validated, accepted/rejected against rules — confirmation in seconds.
  • Customs docsCross-border paperwork pre-filled from PO + commercial invoice. Broker review only on exceptions.
  • Carrier commsStatus update requests, ETA confirmations, exception handling — drafted, sent, logged.
  • POD reconciliationProof-of-delivery matching against shipment records. Auto-close clean ones, flag the rest.
V-06

Professional
Services

Knowledge work in, knowledge work out. The category where leverage compounds fastest.

  • Research synthesisMulti-source research summarized with citations, formatted to your house style.
  • Proposal draftingFirst-pass proposals drafted from win-themes + past work + RFP. Partner edits.
  • Client reportingRecurring client reports auto-drafted from project data; consultant adds judgment.
  • RFP responsesQuestion parsing, draft response from past responses + current capability matrix.
V-07

Retail
& E-commerce

Customer-facing where it pays, back-office where it scales.

  • Supplier commsInbound supplier emails — POs, ASNs, exceptions — parsed and routed to the right buyer.
  • Inventory exceptionsOut-of-pattern movements flagged; explanations drafted from related touchpoints.
  • Customer queriesOrder status, returns, common Qs — drafted, optionally auto-sent. Always logged.
  • Listing copyProduct description drafting from spec sheets, brand-aligned, marketplace-formatted.
