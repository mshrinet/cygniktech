/ Engagement shapes
How they compose

Most clients start with one. Many compose all four.

Diagnostic only
S-01 alone. ~3 weeks. You walk away with a roadmap and decide where to take it next — even if the answer is "not yet" or "not us."
Pilot
S-01 → S-02 on one workflow. 8–12 weeks total. The fastest way to put a working agent into production with measurable ROI.
Full deployment
S-01 → S-02 + S-03 across 2–4 workflows. ~6 months. The configuration that produces a system, not a feature.
Run mode
S-04 ongoing, layered onto any of the above. Monthly retainer keeps everything monitored, optimized, and governed without adding overhead to your team.
